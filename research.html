Anthropic: Pricing the Pre-IPO AI Leader

How do you price a company that has no stock ticker? Anthropic went from $61.5B to $965B in 14 months while selling the same core product. I argue the revenue growth and cost efficiency are real, but the valuation has outrun both, so investors are paying ahead of what the business has proven.
