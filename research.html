Thesis

Position summary 07/25/2026

Anthropic's revenue growth and cost efficiency are real and well supported. Its valuation has grown faster than either one, which means the price investors are paying is currently ahead of what the business has actually proven.

The bullish evidence is genuine: adoption is deep rather than experimental, compute spending runs near 60 percent of revenue against an estimated 75 percent-plus at OpenAI, and the company projected its first profitable quarter unusually early. What the evidence does not support is the size of the jump itself. A 15.7x increase in 14 months is larger than revenue growth alone justifies, so part of that number is investors pricing in a future that has not happened yet.

What would change my mind: healthy gross margins on the AI product itself and evidence of enterprise renewal and expansion would strengthen the case. Significant losses per dollar of revenue, or heavy revenue concentration in a few large customers, would weaken it. The IPO filing is the real test, since public markets can check these numbers against full disclosures instead of investor projections.
